Frequently Asked Questions

Go to Browse Courses and use the Category and Difficulty dropdowns. Then sort by title or price.

Click Add a Course and fill in the title, category, difficulty, duration, and price. The description is optional. If anything is missing or out of range, the form will tell you what to fix.

Durations must be between 1 and 200 hours. Prices cannot be negative. A price of 0 is allowed for free courses.
