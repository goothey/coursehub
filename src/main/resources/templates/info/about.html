About CourseHub

CourseHub is a learning platform where students can browse courses across programming, design, business, and math. Instructors can add new courses, and admins keep the catalogue accurate and up to date.

Each course lists its category, difficulty level, duration, and price, so you can find the right fit for your goals and budget.

What you can do here

  • Browse all courses and filter them by category and difficulty.
  • Sort courses by title or price, in ascending or descending order.
  • Add a new course using the form, with clear messages if something needs fixing.
